## Blackboard grading rubric shown in the supplied screenshots

The visible rubric contains three scored categories, each showing **1.67 possible points (33.33%)**.

### 1. Implementation

**Exemplary — 1.50–1.67**  
Completion of the implementation stage. Includes related design calculations and justification, with rationalization for any modification to the baseline design.

**Satisfactory — 1.17–1.33**  
Implementation mostly complete with minor gaps. Design calculations and justification are present but may lack depth or clarity. Modifications are explained but not fully justified.

**Developing — 0.83–1.00**  
Partial implementation with missing or non-functional components. Limited or incomplete calculations. Weak justification.

**Unsatisfactory — 0.33–0.67**  
Minimal implementation. Major components missing or non-functional. Calculations largely absent. Little justification.

**Unacceptable — 0–0.17**  
No meaningful implementation or justification.

### 2. Validation Experiments

**Exemplary — 1.50–1.67**  
Clear design of validation experiments for testing major parts of the product and the final product to validate the design specifications and performance metrics.

**Satisfactory — 1.17–1.33**  
Validation experiments cover most components. Some connection to specifications and performance metrics.

**Developing — 0.83–1.00**  
Limited or incomplete validation experiments. Weak linkage to specifications and performance metrics.

**Unsatisfactory — 0.33–0.67**  
Poorly defined experiments with minimal testing.

**Unacceptable — 0–0.17**  
No validation experiments.

### 3. Work Plan

**Exemplary — 1.50–1.67**  
Achievable plan for the remaining tasks to complete the project.

**Satisfactory — 1.17–1.33**  
Generally achievable plan with defined tasks and timeline but limited detail.

**Developing — 0.83–1.00**  
Incomplete or overly optimistic plan. Tasks vague.

**Unsatisfactory — 0.33–0.67**  
Poorly defined or unrealistic plan.

**Unacceptable — 0–0.17**  
No work plan.
