The authors have prepared a well-structured and comprehensive report, and they have clearly and effectively explained their project. However, a number of comments have been provided to help improve the quality and clarity of the work.

1. Some objectives are written in terms of methods (e.g., “develop a framework”) rather than focusing on clear and measurable outcomes. 

2. The lower-level objectives do not clearly include quantitative targets such as percentage improvements or specific performance values. 

3. The selection of 45 scenarios is not fully justified, and further explanation is needed to show why this number is sufficient for reliable results. 

4. The impact of changing Grey Wolf Optimization parameters (such as population size and number of iterations) is not discussed. 

5. The accuracy of the ARIMA forecasting model is not validated using standard error metrics such as RMSE or MAPE. 

6. The weights used in the Weighted Sum Method are not clearly justified or supported by sensitivity analysis or decision rationale. 

7. It is assumed that the generated scenarios fully represent real-world conditions, but no comparison with actual historical data is provided.
